# Summary

Pipeline diagnosis follows one signal from production through recording, transport, collection, processing and destination ingestion. Each stage can exclude, delay or reject data. Internal metrics and logs help locate the failing contract but have their own scope. Diagnostic exporters can reveal payloads and should be used with deliberate access and privacy controls. A healthy listener or process does not establish complete end-to-end delivery.
